## 14.3 Evidence levels used in this guide

### Mongolia-specific technical recommendation

Statements directly adapted from the two STREAM/Mendel University sources are identified in the relevant chapter.

### General arboricultural practice

Planting depth, root-flare exposure, broad-hole planting, mulch separation, moisture-based irrigation and structural pruning are presented as general practice. Site adaptation is required.

### Working field range

Water volumes and inspection intervals are explicitly labeled as starting ranges. They are operational planning values, not universal biological constants.

## 14.4 Citation notes

This guide paraphrases its sources. It does not reproduce long copyrighted passages. Users should consult the original documents for figures, context and exact wording.
