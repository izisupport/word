# Chapter 12 - Annual Tree-Care Calendar for Mongolia

Timing varies by location, elevation and year. Use plant and soil conditions rather than calendar date alone.

## January

- inspect winter guards after wind;
- remove dangerous snow loads carefully;
- prevent salt and ash from being dumped in root zones;
- review records and order seed or plants.

## February

- inspect sunscald risk on young trunks;
- repair wind screens;
- plan pruning;
- check stored seed and rodent protection.

## March

- prune suitable dormant deciduous trees during safe weather;
- avoid pruning during extreme cold;
- prepare tools;
- inspect for frost cracks;
- plan irrigation repairs.

## April

- monitor soil thaw;
- do not work saturated soil;
- expose buried root flares;
- repair fences and guards;
- prepare planting sites;
- begin early planting when soil is workable.

## May

- principal planting period in many locations;
- water newly planted stock immediately;
- start moisture logs;
- mulch;
- control early weeds;
- inspect conifer winter injury;
- delay diagnosis of dead wood until bud break clarifies living tissue.

## June

- monitor new growth;
- inspect moisture at least weekly;
- control weeds;
- train young trees lightly;
- inspect aphids and leaf symptoms;
- avoid unnecessary fertilizer.

## July

- deep-water during drought;
- check stakes after storms;
- maintain mulch;
- inspect for borers and fungal problems;
- provide temporary shade to stressed new stock;
- in nursery systems, species-specific root pruning may occur in mid-July onward under suitable conditions.

## August

- continue drought watering;
- avoid late heavy nitrogen;
- complete nursery root-pruning operations only with adequate irrigation;
- assess survival;
- plan autumn replacements;
- collect seed from species that mature at this time, using correct protocols.

## September

- plant suitable hardy deciduous stock early enough for root establishment;
- continue watering during dry weather;
- renew mulch;
- install browse guards;
- stop practices that stimulate soft late growth.

## October

- provide pre-winter deep watering if autumn is dry and drainage is adequate;
- protect vulnerable evergreen and smooth-barked young trees;
- clean diseased debris where appropriate;
- label plants before snow.

## November

- secure guards;
- drain irrigation lines;
- protect stored seed;
- prevent rodent nesting at trunk bases;
- keep mulch away from bark.

## December

- inspect after snow and wind;
- avoid compacting snow repeatedly over root zones;
- review mortality and update next year's species choices.

## Seasonal priority table

| Season | Main priority |
|---|---|
| Spring | Planting, drainage, establishment irrigation |
| Summer | Moisture monitoring, weeds, pests, stake inspection |
| Autumn | Hardening, dry-weather watering, winter protection |
| Winter | Mechanical protection, planning, records |

## Event-based triggers

Regardless of month:

- after strong wind: inspect stakes and lean;
- after heavy rain: inspect ponding;
- after heat wave: check deeper moisture;
- after early frost: delay unnecessary pruning;
- after construction: inspect compaction and root injury;
- after animal entry: inspect bark and leaders.
