# Home Tree Planting and Care in Mongolia

## A practical English-language guide for creating an attractive, healthy and climate-resilient home yard

**Edition:** 1.0  
**Geographic focus:** Mongolia, with particular relevance to Ulaanbaatar, the central steppe, forest-steppe settlements and cold continental residential sites  
**Audience:** Homeowners, community gardeners, landscape installers and small nurseries

## Purpose

This guide explains how to select, plant and care for trees and shrubs in a Mongolian home yard. It combines Mongolian forestry recommendations with general nursery and outplanting principles from FAO, the USDA Forest Service and other forestry institutions.

The guide is intentionally practical. It covers:

- site assessment and yard zoning;
- species selection;
- planting seasons;
- planting-hole preparation;
- bare-root, container and balled-root stock;
- watering;
- mulching and soil care;
- staking and wind protection;
- pruning;
- winter preparation;
- pest and disease prevention;
- propagation and small-scale nursery work;
- a month-by-month maintenance calendar;
- sample planting plans.

## Important limitations

Mongolia has strong local variation in soil, wind, frost, elevation, groundwater and irrigation-water quality. Watering volumes in this guide are **starting ranges**, not fixed universal prescriptions. Always adjust them by checking moisture in the root zone.

Chemical pesticides, herbicides and fungicides must only be used in accordance with current Mongolian registration, label instructions and local professional guidance. The guide prioritizes prevention, sanitation and non-chemical controls.

## Core Mongolian evidence

Two 2024 STREAM/Mendel University publications are the principal Mongolia-specific technical sources:

1. **Sustainable Forest Management: Silviculture Basics - Forest Regeneration, Protection and Tending in Mongolian Light Taiga Forests.**
2. **Forest Nurseries in Mongolia - Recommendations for Increasing the Quality and Quantity of Seedling Production.**

They provide Mongolia-specific guidance on seedling quality, root-system development, planting methods, drought protection, shading, weed control, storage, transport and nursery operation.
